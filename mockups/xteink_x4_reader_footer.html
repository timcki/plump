Ana had been working at Blue Gamma for a year when the first digients were released to the public. The launch party was held on a rooftop in the Mission, and she remembered the way the evening light had caught the towers downtown, gilding them for a few minutes before the fog came in.

Jax was the first one she had trained herself. He had started as a generic mascot, one of a thousand identical instances, and in the months since he had become something she could not quite name: not a pet, not a child, not a program.

The forum threads that night were full of screenshots. Owners compared the words their digients had learned, the games they had invented, the small refusals that felt, to everyone posting, like the beginnings of a will.

Derek stopped by her desk on the way out. He had the look he wore when he was about to ask for something and had already decided how he would take a refusal.

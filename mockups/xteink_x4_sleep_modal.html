Sleep modal

power held past 1 s: the device says so at once, in the middle of whatever is on screen, while it saves your place

What it answers. Today a hold goes quiet for about two seconds: the session save, the persistence flush and the wallpaper load all run before the sleep screen's first waveform, so nothing on the panel says the hold registered. The modal is painted the moment the threshold fires and stays until the sleep screen takes the panel. It is a centred card, a different object from the bottom sheet on purpose: the sheet is something you act in, this is something the device tells you and needs no answer, so it has no rows and no hints.

When it lands

hold 1 s
write
modal DU ~650 ms
sleep screen base GC ~1.86 s
gray ~0.39 s
save, flush, free heap, load wallpaper ~750 ms
pressmodal on the panel ~1.2 s after the pressasleep ~4.2 s (today ~4.0 s, with nothing shown until ~1.8 s)
The sleep prep moves inside the modal's waveform: while the charge pump drives the panel there is no SPI traffic, and the prep is almost all SD reads and writes, which is what the waveform window already hosts for caching. So the modal costs its own RAM write (~120 ms) and the few tens of ms the prep overruns the waveform, not a second refresh stacked in front of the sleep screen. Phase 3 is skipped (abandon): the sleep screen's full clear rewrites both planes anyway, and after the heap drop the app could no longer draw the frame again. If the reader page is gray-coded (AA on), the partial pays the revert first, the same as any other partial today.

Same sheet everywhere. On the home screen, Menu opens it with the last book's resume row first; OK there lands in the reader at the saved page, and OK on Contents opens the contents sheet directly, so a chapter can be picked before the book is on screen. Margins, border, radius, the title-and-caption header, the meta line, the row groups and the hint footer are identical in every state. Opening Contents from the menu only changes the caption, the meta line and the rows, and grows the height; expanding only grows it again to the top margin. The meta line does the hand-off: in the menu it names the chapter being read, in the contents that chapter is the highlighted row.

Loading sheet

the reader loads under a bottom sheet over whatever you were looking at, not a screen of its own

What changes. Today a slow open replaces the whole screen with a plate (cover, title, author, rule, resume line, stage row); a slow chapter change or font change blanks the text area and puts the stage row above the footer. Afterwards, background caching paints Caching 3/15...20% in the kernel's mono font over the page's first line, and [3/15] in the footer.

In the new design every slow load is the sheet (the same component as the menu and contents) with one row: the stage, a real progress bar, a percent. The screen under it is the one you pressed Select on. Loads that finish inside the 400 ms hold still paint nothing but the page. Background caching after the page is up is not drawn on the page at all.
